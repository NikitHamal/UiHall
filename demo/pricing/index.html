Questions

Anything else? Our engineers answer the chat — usually within an hour.

What counts as a run?

One agent invocation from trigger to final output, however many tool calls it makes. Retries of the same run are free.

What happens if I go over?

Nothing breaks. Extra runs are $0.40 per thousand on Pro, and we email you at 80% so it never surprises you.

Can I switch plans later?

Any time. Upgrades are prorated to the day; downgrades apply at the next billing date.

Do you offer discounts for open source?

Yes — Pro is free for maintainers of public repositories with more than 100 stars.
